# Atomic notes make ideas reusable

A note that holds exactly one idea can be linked from many different contexts without dragging unrelated material along. A note that mixes three ideas can only be linked as a bundle, so it gets reused less and becomes harder to find.

## Why it matters
- Small notes are easier to title as a clear claim, which makes the vault searchable by idea.
- They are the building blocks for writing: an essay becomes an ordered sequence of existing notes.
