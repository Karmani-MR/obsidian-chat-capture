# Ahrens - How to Take Smart Notes

## Summary in my own words
Ahrens describes the note-taking system of sociologist Niklas Luhmann (the *Zettelkasten*, or "slip box") and argues that writing is not the last step of thinking but the medium of it. Instead of collecting highlights, you keep three kinds of notes, each with a different lifespan and job.

## Key ideas
- **Fleeting notes** are quick reminders of a thought. They are meant to be thrown away once processed.
- **Literature notes** capture what a source says, briefly and in your own words, with a reference.
- **Permanent notes** are your own ideas, written so they make sense without the original context, and linked to other notes.
- Value comes from **connections** between notes, not from the size of the collection.
